Terms and conditions

For the Hyperliquid India x ENS NFC metal card, House of HYPE, Mumbai.

The cards

There are 25 numbered NFC metal cards, made by Hyperliquid India and ENS. Each one is etched with the name of its holder, a .hl or .eth name. There is one card per wallet, per email and per name: if david.hl has claimed, david.eth cannot, and the other way round.

Claim window

Claims open on 27 October and close on 29 October at 11:59 PM IST.

How to claim

Check your .hl or .eth name on the site. A name needs a score of 80 or more out of 100 to claim, based on public Hyperliquid activity, with the score shown on the site. If your name qualifies, enter the email you used to register on Luma and claim. Qualifying names claim first come, first served until all 25 cards are gone.

Your email

We use your email only to match your claim to the Luma guest list. It is stored privately and never shown on the site.

Collection

Claimants collect their card in person at the House of HYPE event in Mumbai on 3 November.

Proof of ownership

At pickup, the claimant shows proof of the wallet behind their name, so the card goes to the person who owns the name.
